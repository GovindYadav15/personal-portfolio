# Surface brief: Govind's portfolio

## Scope and mode

Single-page web portfolio. Experience.

## Audience and job

Recruiters and engineering teams assess Govind's backend, DevOps, cloud, and software experience, inspect real project evidence, and contact him about opportunities.

## Content and constraints

Keep all current portfolio sections, factual claims, links, project and certificate imagery, CV access, and certificate viewing. Use only the evidence already in the repository; add no metrics, customers, or outcomes. The user requires a minimalistic redesign with dark forest green as the primary color.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Treat the portfolio as a collider event display: each real project is an inspectable signal, and the visitor traces outward from the work to its context and the engineer behind it. Refuse the standard stack of identical project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black forest green carries the broad field; layered pine rings, quiet mineral lines, ivory type, and a restrained chartreuse signal mark structure and selection. Use precise grotesk text with tabular numerals, thin rules, concentric geometry, and squared controls. No fake metrics or simulated measurements.

STORY: Let engineering work lead; move from selected projects to skills, experience, education, credentials, and direct contact. All navigation and focus states remain readable without color or motion.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A narrow top rail holds the wordmark, section navigation, and theme control. Below, Govind's name and role occupy the left half with one sentence and clear project/CV actions. A real portrait anchors the right half inside a fine concentric field; small labels identify it as the portfolio's central subject. The projects heading and first project begin within the initial viewport's lower edge.

FORM: Selected challenger, scientific-notation-particle-detector-event-display; seed 1f3b12bc. Its engineering-specific reading and focused selection beat the assigned couture atelier on audience identification and product clarity. Preserve the collider's concentric topology, isolate-on-selection control, named states, and restrained-responsive geometry, translating its palette to forest green. Signature interaction: selecting a project brings its real details and image into focus while the rest of the field recedes; standard links remain available to everyone.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
