# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and engineering teams evaluating Govind Kumar Yadav for backend, DevOps, and software engineering opportunities.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Govind's background, skills, projects, education, experience, certifications, and contact details so visitors can assess his work and reach out about opportunities or collaboration.

## Positioning

An individual engineering portfolio that brings backend, DevOps, cloud, and software project work together with the owner's experience and direct contact channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page portfolio with in-page navigation across home, about, skills, projects, experience, certifications, and contact sections.
- Visitors can open the CV PDF and view certificate images.
- Project links, email, GitHub, and LinkedIn are presented as contact or work destinations.
- Built with React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion.
- Preserve existing factual claims and links unless Govind supplies corrections.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name and owner: Govind Kumar Yadav.
- The user requests a minimalistic redesign with dark forest green as the primary color.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project descriptions and screenshots in `src/pages/Projects.jsx` and `public/projects/`.
- Work and education histories in `src/pages/Experience.jsx` and `src/pages/About.jsx`, with organization marks in `public/experience/`.
- Certification titles and certificate images in `src/pages/Certifications.jsx` and `public/certificates/`.
- Owner portrait at `public/details/govind_yadav.jpg` and CV at `public/details/Govind_Kr_Yadav_CV.pdf`.
- Contact channels and profile links in `src/pages/Contact.jsx` and `src/components/Footer.jsx`.
- No testimonials or independently verified outcome metrics are present; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Make Govind's engineering focus clear to a hiring audience.
- Let real projects and experience support evaluation.
- Keep contact and CV access straightforward.
- Preserve the accuracy of supplied career and project details.
